# My Journey of Learning Blender and 3D Modeling

I started learning Blender because I was interested in 3D art. At first, Blender was difficult because it had many tools, buttons, and shortcuts. However, I watched beginner videos and practiced step by step.

## My First Steps

I began with simple shapes such as cubes, balls, and cylinders. I learned how to move, turn, resize, and join them. Later, I learned how to create simple objects, add colors and textures, set up lights, and make final images.

## Challenges and Progress

Sometimes, my models did not look good, and I made many mistakes. However, these mistakes helped me learn. With regular practice, I became more confident and improved my skills.

## My Future Goals

Learning Blender has taught me to be patient and creative. I can now turn some of my ideas into 3D models. I still have a lot to learn, but I am happy with my progress. In the future, I hope to create better models and learn more about animation.
